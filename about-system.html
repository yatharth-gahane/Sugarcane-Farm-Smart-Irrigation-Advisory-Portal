Objectives
  1. To monitor soil moisture levels in the sugarcane field using soil-moisture sensors.
  2. To determine the irrigation requirement of sugarcane based on soil moisture, crop growth stage, weather conditions, and water availability.
  3. To provide timely irrigation recommendations to farmers, helping them decide when and how much to irrigate.
  4. To reduce water wastage by avoiding unnecessary or excessive irrigation.
  5. To improve sugarcane growth and yield by maintaining suitable soil moisture throughout different crop-growth stages.
  6. To integrate weather information such as rainfall, temperature, and humidity into irrigation decisions.
  7. To automate irrigation when required by controlling pumps or irrigation valves based on the system's recommendations.
  8. To provide real-time monitoring and alerts through a mobile or web-based interface.
  9. To maintain irrigation records and analyze water usage for better farm management.
  10. To promote efficient and sustainable water management in sugarcane cultivation.

  2. Reduces water wastage
